Professionally trained at VLCC Institute, Jayanagar, Bangalore, I specialize in translating each client's unique beauty into a curated look — whether it's an intimate mehendi morning or a grand bridal evening.

My practice spans HD & long-wear bridal makeup, advanced skin therapies, creative hair color, and immaculate saree draping. Every look is built around face structure, skin tone, and the story you want to tell.

I bring a calm, hygienic, and deeply personalized experience to every appointment — because beauty is as much about how you feel as how you look.
